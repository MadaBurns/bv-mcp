### Changed

- `scan_domain` no longer persists a `cache:...:check:<category>` KV entry for each of its scored and web-posture checks, nor reads them. A cold scan now makes one SCAN_CACHE write (the scan-level result, unchanged 5-minute TTL and `force_refresh` behaviour) instead of roughly 27. Direct `check_*` calls keep their own per-check cache. Concurrent scans of one domain in an isolate still share each in-flight check.
- A `scan_domain` call that must rescan because the cached scan lacks the web posture section now re-runs the checks rather than reusing per-check entries.
