### Fixed

- Tenant weekly rescan: domains that are scanned but never graded keep no `last_scanned_at` (SQ-496), so they sorted first in every tick's 500-domain batch. A tenant with 500 or more of them never reached its other watched domains. The batch is now ordered by the later of `last_scanned_at` and `fingerprint_at`, which the dispatcher refreshes for every due domain it inspects, so inspected domains move behind ones not yet inspected. Stamping and grades are unchanged: an ungraded scan still writes no stamp, score or grade.
