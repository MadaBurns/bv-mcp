### Fixed

- `check_dbl`: a blocklist zone that answers with a rate-limit or access stub (Spamhaus `127.255.255.x`, URIBL `0x01`) or an unrecognised return code is no longer counted as a "not listed" verdict (#1197, #900). The unqualified "Domain not listed on any blocklist" is emitted only when every zone returned a usable verdict; otherwise the claim is bounded to the zones that did ("No listings on the zones that answered", `low`, with `zonesChecked` / `unansweredZones` / `quotaLimited` / `unrecognizedResponses`). Zero answered zones, or zero usable verdicts across answered zones, now abstain (`checkStatus: 'error'`, score 0, `passed: false`, `partial: true`) instead of pinning a fabricated clean reputation in the 3600 s cache. Spamhaus `127.255.255.252/.254/.255` are decoded separately (`stubKind: 'typo' | 'public_resolver' | 'excessive_queries'`); `.254` is the registry refusing queries from public resolvers, which is persistent from a DoH vantage, and the finding says so.

Score impact: `dbl` is an intelligence-only category (not in the scan score). Direct callers see `low` instead of `info` for a URIBL stub and an abstention instead of a pass when no zone gave a verdict.
