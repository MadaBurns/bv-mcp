### Added

- A scan-path canary on the existing 15-minute cron: one cache-bypassing `scan_domain` of a fixed control domain (default `isc.org`, override `SCAN_CANARY_DOMAIN`, disable with `SCAN_CANARY_ENABLED=false`). It pages critically on the second consecutive unhealthy run (threw, timed out, ungraded or too little evidence), suppresses repeats, and sends a recovery notice on the first healthy run, so a scan path broken end to end is caught even when traffic is below the alert lanes' sample floor. It never asserts a score or grade, logs its outcome every tick, and documents both variables in `docs/operator-runbook.md`.
