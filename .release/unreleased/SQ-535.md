### Fixed

- `scan_domain`: a domain with a null MX and an SPF policy that authorizes no sender (`v=spf1 -all`) neither sends nor receives mail, and revoking every DKIM key (`v=DKIM1; p=`, often a wildcard `*._domainkey`) is the correct posture for it. The scan already reported the dkim category as N/A for such a domain, but still listed "DKIM keys revoked" (or a per-selector "Revoked DKIM key") as HIGH. Those findings are now `info` and marked not applicable. This is score-bearing under scoring model 1.42.0: report-level N/A does not remove DKIM from canonical scoring, so `non_mail` and explicit profiles with nonzero DKIM weight can gain score or grade; default `web_only` has zero DKIM weight and its overall score is unchanged. A synthetic real-check/post-processing regression moves DKIM 75 to 100 and the `non_mail` overall score 81/B to 90/A. The standalone check and dns-checks package shipping inputs are unchanged. A usable key keeps its rating, including a weak one, and so does a domain that receives mail or authorizes a sender.
