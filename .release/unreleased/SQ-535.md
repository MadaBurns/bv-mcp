### Fixed

- `scan_domain`: a domain with a null MX and an SPF policy that authorizes no sender (`v=spf1 -all`) neither sends nor receives mail, and revoking every DKIM key (`v=DKIM1; p=`, often a wildcard `*._domainkey`) is the correct posture for it. The scan already reported the dkim category as N/A for such a domain, but still listed "DKIM keys revoked" (or a per-selector "Revoked DKIM key") as HIGH. Those findings are now `info` and marked not applicable. No grade changes, because the category is not scored for these domains. A usable key keeps its rating, including a weak one, and so does a domain that receives mail or authorizes a sender.
