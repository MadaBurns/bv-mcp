### Added

- Authenticated keys now have a daily total across all tools, on top of the existing per-tool limits: agent 2,000, developer 5,000 and enterprise 50,000 calls/day (`TIER_AGGREGATE_DAILY_LIMITS`). The first call past the total gets HTTP 429 (`-32029`, "… requests per day across all tools"). Owner, partner and free keys, and unauthenticated callers, are not affected. The total is counted only for calls that pass session validation and the concurrency limit, and an allowed call on a capped tier carries `x-quota-total-remaining`. A `tools/call` naming the reserved counter `__all_tools__` gets the unknown-tool result (SQ-520).
