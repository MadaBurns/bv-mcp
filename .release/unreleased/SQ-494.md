### Added

- Authenticated keys now have a daily total across all tools, on top of the existing per-tool limits: agent 2,000, developer 5,000 and enterprise 50,000 calls/day (`TIER_AGGREGATE_DAILY_LIMITS`). The first call past the total gets HTTP 429 (`-32029`, "… requests per day across all tools"). Owner, partner and free keys, and unauthenticated callers, are not affected. The values are starting points pending operator sign-off.
