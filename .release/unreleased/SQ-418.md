### Added

- OAuth: the RFC 7523 `jwt-bearer` grant for Enterprise Managed Auth (US-14). `/oauth/token` now accepts `grant_type=urn:ietf:params:oauth:grant-type:jwt-bearer` with an Identity Assertion JWT Authorization Grant (ID-JAG, `typ: oauth-id-jag+jwt`, RS256/ES256) signed by an IdP that an EMA tenant allowlists. The assertion's issuer must belong to an enabled tenant whose entitlement has not lapsed, the requesting `client_id` must be on that tenant's allowlist and resolve (a registered or CIMD client), the assertion's `client_id` claim must match it, the signature must verify against the tenant's JWKS (refetched once on an unknown `kid`), `aud` must be this server's issuer (a string or a one-element array), the lifetime at most one hour within 30 s of skew, and each `jti` is accepted once. A `resource` parameter or claim must name this server's `/mcp` URL (`invalid_target` otherwise). The response grants scope `mcp` only, at the tenant's tier (never owner), clamped to the tenant entitlement, with `Cache-Control: no-store` and no `refresh_token`. Authorization-server metadata now lists the grant in `grant_types_supported` and advertises `authorization_grant_profiles_supported: ["urn:ietf:params:oauth:grant-profile:id-jag"]`.

### Changed

- `/internal/oauth/grants` (bv-web paid consent) now resolves Client ID Metadata Document clients and matches their loopback redirects with the port ignored, the same way `/oauth/authorize` does; the bv-web entitlement request accepts a `clientId` up to 512 characters (was 200). The `authorization_code` token exchange is unchanged.
