### Fixed

- `batch_scan_status` and `batch_scan_findings` now report an async batch job as `failed` (with `error: "consumer_abandoned"`) when it is still queued or running but has not been updated for 12 minutes, which is longer than every queue delivery attempt and retry delay combined. Previously a job whose consumer was killed on every delivery stayed `running` until its result expired. Jobs inside the bound, finished jobs, and jobs of other principals are unchanged.
