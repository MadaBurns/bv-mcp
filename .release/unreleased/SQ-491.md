### Changed

- bv-ct-index sidecar: a T0 -> T1 compaction job now closes its output at a content-chosen subset of the cuts, at most `MAX_T1_RANGES` (128) runs a job instead of one per cut range (~940). That means fewer R2 puts and D1 inserts per job. The hourly roll-up, T1 -> T2 and T3 still write at every cut, and reads return the same rows. Ships with `npm run deploy:ct-index`, not `deploy:prod`.
