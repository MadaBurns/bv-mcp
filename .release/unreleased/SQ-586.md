### Added

- Optional `OAUTH_SIGNING_SECRET_PREVIOUS` secret. During a rotation it lets access tokens signed with the retired secret keep verifying (verify only; nothing is ever signed with it), so replacing `OAUTH_SIGNING_SECRET` no longer signs every customer out at once. The current secret is tried first; every other check (algorithm, expiry, issuer, audience, token version, revocation) is unchanged. Unset, blank, shorter than 32 bytes, or equal to the current secret means rotation is off (the last two log one fixed-token warning per isolate). It is listed in the security-critical secret inventory and is never required for deploy. Rotation procedure: operator runbook section 8.
