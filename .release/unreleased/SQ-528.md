### Changed

- bv-ct-index sidecar: the T0 indexer now stores the `key` and `apex` it derives from each queued name (`nameKey`, `apexOf`), and derives a sibling row's key from its two apexes. A queued `key` or `apex` may now be omitted. When present it must agree with the derived value, or the row is refused. This is deploy 1 of 2 for the queue-cost fix in #1341. The tailer still sends the full shape, so this change alone does not lower queue operations. Dropping the fields in the tailer is a separate, later deploy, and it must ship after this one.

### Fixed

- bv-ct-index sidecar: a queued `apex` was stored as received, so a forged value could be persisted and could win a merge tie-break. The indexer now refuses a row whose apex disagrees with `apexOf(name)`, a name with no registrable apex (as the tailer does), an issuer longer than the tailer's 256-character cut, and a sibling row whose sides are not distinct registrable apexes. It stores sibling apexes in folded form.
