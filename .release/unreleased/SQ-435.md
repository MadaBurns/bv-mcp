### Security

- Enterprise Managed Auth tenant-wide revocation. Disabling, deleting or re-creating an EMA tenant, letting its `entitlement_expires_at` pass, or changing its tier now cuts off that tenant's EXISTING access tokens within KV propagation (about 60 s) instead of at their expiry (up to 1 hour). Every EMA access token carries a new `tep` claim, the tenant's token epoch at mint, and each `/mcp` request with an `ema:` subject re-reads the sealed tenant record: a missing, unreadable, disabled, lapsed, tier-mismatched or epoch-mismatched tenant (or a token with no `tep`) gets the normal 401 challenge, and a tenant-store read failure gets the retryable 503. The epoch is the new server-managed `token_epoch` field (epoch ms, set by `PUT /internal/oauth/ema-tenants` on create and on an `enabled` true→false transition, and rejected in the request body); records without it use `created_at`. Re-enabling a tenant does not revive tokens minted before its suspension. Non-EMA bearers do no extra I/O.
