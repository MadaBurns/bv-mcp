### Fixed

- The tenant scan-queue consumer now stamps `domains.last_scanned_at`, `last_score` and `last_grade` once a scan row is stored. Nothing wrote these columns before. Every watched domain therefore looked never-scanned, so each weekly tick rescanned the same first 500 domains, never reached the rest, and never used the unchanged-fingerprint skip. A tenant with 600 domains is now fully scanned over two ticks. A dead-lettered domain is not stamped, so it stays due.
- The weekly due and stale checks allow 6 hours of slack. A scan finishing minutes after its Sunday tick no longer pushes the domain's next check out by a whole week.
