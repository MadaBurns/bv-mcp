### Changed

- `map_compliance` now carries a fixed `scopeNotice` on every result, in both the text output and the structured output, stating that the mapping reflects only what is observable from public DNS and HTTP, indicates control posture for the listed control ids, and is not an audit, certification or attestation. Totals and per-control verdicts are unchanged.
