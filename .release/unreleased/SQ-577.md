### Fixed

- `batch_scan_start` now applies the same stale-job deadline as `batch_scan_status`. A queued or running job past `ASYNC_BATCH_STALE_AFTER_MS` is treated as failed when its idempotency key is reused, so a new job starts instead of handing back a dead one until someone polled it. A live job inside the bound is still replayed. The integrator guide no longer tells callers to poll before reusing a key.
