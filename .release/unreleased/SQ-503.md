### Fixed

- Brand audit (`discovery_mode: tiered`): a failed tier-1 `bv-infrastructure-graph` lookup used to show only `tier1Status: "degraded"`, so a dead upstream could not be told apart from a slow one or a schema change. The audit's `tiers` block now carries `tier1DegradedReason`, for example `http_503:optout_service_unavailable`, `timeout`, `schema_mismatch`, `body_unreadable`, `fetch_failed` or `no_internal_key`. A lookup cut off by the 5 s deadline now reports `tier1Status: "timeout"` instead of `degraded`. Producer error codes are passed through only when they are plain snake_case tokens.
