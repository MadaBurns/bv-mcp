### Fixed

- Brand audit (`discovery_mode: tiered`): a failed tier-1 `bv-infrastructure-graph` lookup used to show only `tier1Status: "degraded"`, so a dead upstream could not be told apart from a slow one or a schema change. The audit's `tiers` block now carries `tier1DegradedReason`: `timeout`, `fetch_failed`, `body_unreadable`, `schema_mismatch`, `lookup_rejected`, or `upstream_unavailable` for every other cause. Producer HTTP codes and configuration-state reasons are mapped to `upstream_unavailable` before they reach a caller, and the exact token is logged (SQ-524). A lookup cut off by the 5 s deadline, including a response body that stalls after its headers, now reports `tier1Status: "timeout"` instead of `degraded`.
