### Changed

- The six non-scoring `scan_domain` web posture checks now follow the same rule as the scored checks: they share an in-flight run but write nothing to the per-check cache (KV or in-memory). Previously they still wrote per-check entries into the in-memory fallback cache, which the single scan-level entry already makes redundant.
- `scan_domain` with `force_refresh` no longer refreshes cached results of direct `check_*` calls. A direct check can therefore return its cached result for up to its TTL after a forced scan of the same domain; pass `force_refresh` on the direct call to bypass it.
