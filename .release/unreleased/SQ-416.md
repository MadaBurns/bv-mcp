### Added

- Enterprise Managed Auth (EMA) per-tenant trusted-issuer store and operator seeding route. `PUT/GET /internal/oauth/ema-tenants` and `GET/DELETE /internal/oauth/ema-tenants/:tenant_id` register the IdP issuers a tenant trusts (issuer normalized, one issuer per tenant, `jwks_uri` resolved from the issuer's OIDC discovery document when omitted), gated by the existing `BV_MCP_OAUTH_MINT_KEY`; records are sealed in `SESSION_STORE` when `KV_ENVELOPE_KEY` is set. Nothing consumes the store yet (the jwt-bearer grant lands separately), so there is no behavior change for existing clients. No migration, no new secret; the runbook has a "Seeding an Enterprise Managed Auth tenant" section.
