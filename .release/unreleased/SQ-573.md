### Fixed

- `check_dane_https`, `check_svcb_https` and `check_ptr` no longer report a missing record when the resolver failed to answer. A SERVFAIL or REFUSED answer for the TLSA, HTTPS, MX or mail-host A lookup now produces an inconclusive result (`errorKind: dns_error`) that scoring excludes, instead of "No DANE TLSA for HTTPS", "No HTTPS record found", "PTR not applicable (no inbound mail)" or "Mail server IPs unresolved". Answered NOERROR-empty and NXDOMAIN lookups keep their findings and scores.
- `check_bimi` no longer reports a published BIMI record as "ineffective (DMARC not enforcing)" when the `_dmarc` lookup itself went unanswered; it reports the prerequisite as not determined.

### Changed

- Scoring model 2.1.0 to 2.2.0 and `@blackveil/dns-checks` 2.1.1 to 2.2.0. The model entry also records the `http_security` change that shipped in dns-checks 2.1.1: an HTTP status 0 or 5xx answer is unmeasured and excluded from the score. Which scans are affected, and how often, is not measured against the corpus. No weight, tier, grade band, severity penalty, missing-control rule or profile-detection rule changed. (SQ-573)
